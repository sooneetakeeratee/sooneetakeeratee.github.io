Ask Handmade Ask Mikki Fung
How long does a handmade order take?

Most handmade pieces — crochet, candles and bouquets — are ready in about 3 days. Custom or larger orders can take a little longer; we'll confirm the timing when you order.

Where do you ship?

We ship worldwide — including Hong Kong, the UK and the USA. Send a message with your country and we'll confirm the delivery details.

How long does delivery take?

Usually 1 to 2 weeks (7–15 days) after your order is posted, depending on the country.

How do I pay?

Payment is by bank transfer. We'll send the account details by DM once your order is confirmed.

Do you take custom orders?

Yes! Tell us the colours, flowers, scent or occasion you have in mind and we'll make something just for you.

How much do things cost?

Prices start from Rs 500 and depend on the product and size. Message us with what you like and we'll give you an exact price.

Can I visit the Mikki Fung shop?

Yes — the shop is at Pipalbot, Dhumbarahi, Kathmandu 44600. Open in Maps

  • Sunday – Friday10 AM – 7 PM
  • SaturdayClosed

Hours may change on festival days — send a DM before visiting.

Can I return or exchange an item?

All sales are final — we're not able to offer returns or exchanges. If you're unsure about a size, colour or detail, please ask us before you order and we'll happily send more photos.

About

“It started with a hook, a length of yarn, and a quiet evening.”

— Sooneeta

A crocheted flower that didn't need to exist. A candle poured for a friend. A bouquet wrapped for someone's birthday, an outfit put together just so, a small moment worth filming. None of it was a plan — it was just what I loved doing.

Those little joys grew into three brands, all of them mine. Some of it I make by hand. Some of it I choose because I'd wear it myself. Either way, I'll get it to you wherever you are, with the same belief behind it: that the things we keep around us should feel warm, honest, and a little bit personal.
