About

“It started with a hook, a length of yarn, and a quiet evening.”

— Sooneeta

A crocheted flower that didn't need to exist. A candle poured for a friend. A bouquet wrapped for someone's birthday, an outfit put together just so, a small moment worth filming. None of it was a plan — it was just what I loved doing.

Those little joys grew into three brands, all of them mine. Some of it I make by hand. Some of it I choose because I'd wear it myself. Either way, I'll get it to you wherever you are, with the same belief behind it: that the things we keep around us should feel warm, honest, and a little bit personal.
